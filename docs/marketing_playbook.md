# Karmora marketing playbook

Distilled from research on @aschapmann (Axel Schapmann, founder of Redship) —
what worked for his build-in-public run, where he fell short, and how Karmora
can capitalize.

## Context snapshot (as of research)

- ~8.5K followers, 600+ posts
- Shipped 7 projects in 2022
- Started English X account end-2024 (originally French-speaking)
- Redship at ~€583/mo (Mar 2026), MyFeedIn at €10K+ total
- Strong SEO play via Reddit + personal blog
- Substack + X combo

## What worked for him

- **Public MRR journaling with exact numbers.** "Day 1 of growing RedShip to
  $1000 MRR" style. Monthly revenue breakdowns (e.g. "March: 2,523€ total,
  RedShip 583€") — specificity builds trust.
- **Stakes-based accountability hooks.** Bet his brother 100€ he'd hit 500
  followers by June — turned personal stake into shareable narrative.
- **Substack-as-long-form anchor.** Reflective essays feed X content and rank
  on Google. Examples: *"To the conquest of Twitter"*, *"From 0 to €10K: the
  slow growth of MyFeedIn"*.
- **SEO via his own blog on redship.io.** Tactical titles ("Reddit marketing
  mistakes", "Best RedReach alternatives") — 2,000 SEO clicks in 4 months.
  Content = funnel, not fluff.
- **Dogfooding narrative.** Used Reddit (via his own tool Redship) to market
  Redship — the product IS the story.
- **Pricing experiments posted openly.** Killed free trial → hard paywall →
  shared the conversion lift. Pricing changes = content.
- **Permission to be cringe early.** Openly said he was bad at X at first,
  no audience = safe to experiment. Reps > polish.

## What he missed / weak spots

- **Follower growth is slow.** ~14 followers/post across 600+ posts. Many
  posts "barely get likes" by his own admission.
- **No signature viral tweet format.** No recurring hook, no visual template,
  no meme.
- **Weak native thread game.** Mostly single-posts and Substack links.
  Leans outbound instead of native X retention.
- **7 projects in 2022 = scattered.** Dilutes narrative. No single
  compounding story arc until Redship.
- **English account started late (end-2024).** Left years of audience-building
  on the table.
- **Limited founder-identity branding.** Bio/pinned doesn't hammer one clear
  position.
- **Underuses screenshots/UI demos.** Scanner-type products thrive on
  visuals; he leans text.

## Karmora playbook (ranked by impact)

1. **Ship a "Scan of the Day" tweet format.** Pick a well-known site/repo,
   run Karmora, post the redacted findings as a screenshot: "Scanned [Big
   SaaS]. Found 3 exposed env vars, 12 SEO issues, 1 CVE. Here's what every
   founder should check." Weekly cadence. This is the viral loop — Axel
   never cracked one, and scanner output is inherently screenshot-bait.
2. **Public MRR + scan-count dashboard.** Steal his transparency play but
   add a second metric he doesn't have: "Today: €X MRR, Y sites scanned,
   Z vulns found." Dual-axis story.
3. **Pick ONE identity and hammer it.** Axel diluted across 7 projects.
   You are "@julezrz, building Karmora" — nothing else in bio, pinned, or
   first 20 tweets of anyone's first visit.
4. **Thread weekly, not link out.** Axel loses retention sending people to
   Substack. Native X threads (7–10 posts) on "10 vulns I found scanning
   indie SaaS this week" keep attention on-platform and compound the algo.
5. **Steal his SEO play, aim at your ICP.** Blog on karmora.com: "npm audit
   alternatives", "how to scan your SaaS for exposed secrets", "SEO issues
   that tank indie launches". Rank, then tweet each post. Axel got 2K
   clicks in 4 months — scanner terms have even higher intent.
6. **Reply-guy to security + indie hacker accounts.** Axel underuses
   replies. Per Julan's own impression playbook (Tarang), parent reach >
   follower count. Target @levelsio, @marc_louvion, @dannypostmaa and
   security researchers with factual 1-sentence scanner findings.
7. **"I scanned 100 indie SaaS, here's what I found" anchor thread.**
   Single flagship thread that becomes the pinned — the kind of post Axel
   never built. Data + screenshots + ICP pain.
8. **Stakes hook, but bigger.** His 100€-with-brother bet was small. Try:
   "If Karmora doesn't hit 50 paying users by [date], I delete the account."
   Public stake = people root for you.
9. **Free public scan tool as lead gen.** Karmora Lite — free scan of any
   URL, gated at results. Tweet the tool, not the SaaS. Axel's hard paywall
   worked post-setup; ours should hook with free output then wall the deep
   report.
10. **Avoid his scatter.** No side projects in public until Karmora hits
    €1K MRR. Every tweet ladders to the scanner.
